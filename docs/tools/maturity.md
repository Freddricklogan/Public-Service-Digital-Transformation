# Digital Maturity Model — gap analysis

Steps 1 and 4 of the [maturity model's assessment process](../digital-maturity-model.md#assessment-process): rate each of the seven dimensions at its current level (1–5, matching the level descriptions) and at the level you are aiming for, and compare. The seven dimensions and the five levels are the model's. The model says the goal is not Level 5 everywhere, so this page reports gaps and does not grade them; the only convention it adds is the order of the list — largest gap first, ties in the model's dimension order.

Nothing you enter leaves your browser.
