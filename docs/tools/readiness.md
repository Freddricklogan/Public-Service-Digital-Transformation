# Stakeholder Readiness — score

Section 4 of the [stakeholder readiness assessment template](../stakeholder-assessment.md#section-4-readiness-summary): the five dimensions scored 1–5 in Section 3, weighted 15 / 30 / 20 / 20 / 15, and classified as Champion (4.0–5.0), Supporter (3.0–3.9), Neutral (2.0–2.9) or Skeptic (1.0–1.9). Weights and bands are the template's; the page only does the arithmetic (rounded to two decimals before classification).
